# Keep distribution assisted and audience-first

The operator may qualify platforms, prepare factual listing packets, and fill
reversible browser fields, but it treats backlinks as incidental and never
creates accounts or makes public submissions autonomously. Exact identity and
action-time confirmation are required because unattended backlink campaigns
invite spam-policy violations, account mistakes, fabricated authority, and
irreversible public actions; a dated, revalidated platform registry preserves
useful launch assistance without encoding changing sites as permanent truth.
