# Separate AI visibility observations and decide on stable windows

The operator collects available AI-assisted search evidence during daily
monitoring but normally selects AI-specific content work during the weekly
cycle from complete comparable periods, using 28 days as an adaptable default.
Google generative impressions, Bing citations and grounding queries, attributed
referrals and conversions, crawler eligibility, and prompt samples remain
separate provider observations instead of becoming a universal AI rank. Content
may be improved as coherent reader-useful answer units, but the operator rejects
fixed token chunks, one-page-per-prompt variants, and repetition for scale. This
trades faster reactions for lower noise, clearer attribution, and less risk of
turning proprietary retrieval theories into site-wide publishing rules.
