# Report contract

Lead with the outcome and distinguish evidence from inference.

Report:

- primary landing page and business metric;
- evidence period and final complete provider date;
- recent intelligence topic, retrieval time, age, source coverage, and artifact;
- GSC property and availability state;
- Google generative-search inclusion and report availability, with impressions
  by supported dimensions when available;
- Bing AI citation, cited-page, grounding-query, and trend availability;
- attributed AI referrals and conversions, crawler eligibility, and sampled
  prompt method when used;
- the top ranked opportunities, especially positions 5 to 20 and comparable
  weak-CTR pages;
- blockers fixed or still open;
- pages proposed, drafted, refreshed, approved, published, and live-verified;
- metadata, internal links, CTA, schema, sitemap, and media changes;
- build and validation results;
- Google sitemap and URL Inspection state;
- IndexNow and optional Bing submission state;
- experiments and backlink/entity audit state;
- applicable practitioner check IDs, verification state, and any rejected
  unsupported constants or causal claims;
- machine-readable manifest and output hashes, changed or conflicting files,
  and live state for `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, and `ai.txt`;
- files changed, review artifact, commit, and deployment state when applicable;
- policy mode, authorization source, action budget, and rollback state;
- quota problems, approvals needed, and next queue.

Use `unavailable` for missing data, not zero. Use `submitted` only for an
acknowledged provider submission, not as a synonym for indexed. Use `verified`
only for a live or provider-confirmed check.

Never combine impressions, citations, prompt samples, rankings, referrals, and
conversions into an undocumented universal AI score. State the provider,
surface, evidence period, denominator, and limitations for each measurement.

Do not report vanity output counts without their quality and business context.
