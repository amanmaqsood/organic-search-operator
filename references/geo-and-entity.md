# GEO and entity guidance

Use these checks when the project asks for visibility in AI-assisted search,
answer engines, or generated search experiences.

## Meaning

GEO in this skill means improving the clarity, accessibility, evidence, and
entity consistency of material that answer systems may retrieve or cite. It is
not a submission protocol and cannot guarantee a mention or citation.

## Checks

- State the brand, product, audience, purpose, limitations, pricing model, and
  important relationships consistently across canonical pages.
- Put important factual answers in crawlable page content rather than only in
  client-side interactions, images, or inaccessible widgets.
- Use descriptive headings, direct answers, tables, definitions, and structured
  sections when they help a reader. Do not format every paragraph for machines.
- Cite primary sources for externally verifiable claims. Keep publication and
  revision dates accurate.
- Maintain clear About, product, contact, policy, author, and methodology pages
  when the business and topic need them.
- Use eligible structured data that agrees with visible content. Structured data
  is not an authority substitute.
- Keep entity names, URLs, organization details, profiles, and sameAs references
  accurate. Do not create fake profiles or mentions.
- Make important pages fast, accessible, internally linked, and available to
  crawlers the site intentionally permits.
- Add transcripts or textual summaries for useful original video and audio.
- Preserve original data, methods, examples, and limitations that make the page
  worth citing.
- Create and maintain the
  [machine-readable discovery set](machine-readable-discovery.md) from the same
  verified canonical content. Treat it as optional-consumer accessibility, not
  a Google ranking factor or a substitute for crawler access.

## Measurement

Track evidence separately:

- search clicks and conversions from Google and Bing;
- qualified referrals from AI-assisted services when analytics identify them;
- reproducible sampled prompts, answer date, locale, model or surface, cited
  URLs, and observed brand wording;
- branded search demand and earned mentions;
- conversions assisted by those visits.

Sampled prompts are observations, not stable rankings. Do not aggregate them
into a universal visibility score unless the method, prompt set, locale, and
sampling limitations are documented.

## Boundaries

Do not create off-site spam, fake consensus, copied community answers, fabricated
citations, or pages whose only purpose is to repeat every prompt variation. Do
not change crawler access without understanding the site's policy and obtaining
approval.
